# Approved GlobeTrek implementation

Accepted 29 October 2026: ten countries (IN JP TH ID FR IT ES GR TR US), each with 3 cities, 12 attractions, 6 stays, 6 tours, 2 editable templates and 2 separately bookable fixed packages.

One country per 1–30-day trip, 1–12 travelers, 365-day horizon. Guests build locally; accounts save owned revisioned drafts. Attractions are informational. Selected stay/tour reservations are atomic, payment simulated, 10-minute quotes and holds, one pending hold per account. Packages share underlying inventory. Confirmation snapshots are immutable. Whole cancellation before earliest item start. No transport inventory, real payments, in-app LLM, reviews/admin or public sharing in this release.

Milestones: protect foundation; globe/theme; destination catalog/maps; trip workspace; templates/packages; unified reservations; hardening/evidence. Progress must distinguish implemented, tested, visually reviewed and deployment-approved. No automatic commits or publication.
