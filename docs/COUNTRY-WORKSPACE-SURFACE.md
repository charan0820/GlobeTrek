# Country relief workspace

## Approved direction contract

THESIS: A country is the shared geographic workspace for discovering places and arranging a trip. The globe approach is a connected camera transition; it does not pretend to morph exactly into a regional projection.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve the living atlas identity, ivory and charcoal-green themes, rust/copper actions, Allura wordmark, Cormorant editorial headings and Source Sans operational text. Real elevation and sourced photography supply the detail. No fictional mountains or decorative settlements.

STORY: Select on the globe, enter a country, inspect a place, choose dates and travelers, arrange days, save and review. Packages remain a separate fixed-product checkout. A price estimate never reserves inventory.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact introduction and Explore/Build your trip controls. Left context or day rail, central country model and right collection/details. Mobile shows the model before its collection, with a usable List alternative. Header notice wraps naturally; the theme target is 44px at top-right.

FORM: Code-led extension of the approved atlas workspace. No new aesthetic selection or generated design comp. Original user choices: geographic relief; isolated country; one workspace; unit plus selection estimates; labelled distant-region insets. No new seed or detector was available because the Impeccable engine is not installed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Acceptance scope

Ten countries: IN, JP, TH, ID, FR, IT, ES, GR, TR, US. All receive real offline-generated GLB relief, mobile variants, geographic pins, regional transforms and source records. Target under 100,000/25,000 triangles and 3MB/1MB per desktop/mobile GLB. Measure metadata separately.

Single globe click selects and raises. Double click, a country-list link and the preview link enter through a 600ms approach; destination reveal is 200ms. Modified links, reduced motion and a failed globe retain ordinary navigation. Duplicate activation is ignored and newer destinations supersede pending entry.

Explore and Plan preserve the trip reducer, selected day, local recovery, ownership and revision-aware saving. Model, Detailed map and List are presentation choices. Only the active geographic engine mounts. Old planner, hotel and product URLs enter the workspace. Back removes the details panel before leaving the country. Guest replacement requires an explicit dialog; packages never enter a custom trip.

Models use finer Natural Earth boundaries and Mapzen Terrarium elevation, with attribution. Insets state that spacing is not geographic distance. Pins preserve coordinates, use elevation metadata and share the mesh projection. Stops keep occurrence IDs and day colors. Routes remain separate by day; cross-inset segments are explicitly described. Orbit cannot go underneath; pan/zoom/reset/fit/region controls have buttons. Ordinary wheel scrolling works; model zoom requires Ctrl/Command. Failed terrain leaves map and list journeys intact.

Pricing beside names distinguishes room/night, per-traveler, component-derived packages, free template copying and unsold attraction tickets. Read-only estimates use server quantity and fee rules, native currency and optional dated INR, with no availability promise. Date/party/room changes change the query identity. Missing data is explained; zero is legitimate. Existing quote/hold/idempotency/expiry/cancellation behavior remains protected.

## Review boundaries

Review against this approved extension and the two user reference ideas, not against a generated comp. No paid services, commits, pushes, parent-folder writes or deployment. Existing photography retains its previous licenses, including the noncommercial Nea Chora exception; no new raster illustration is introduced.
